SCAR-Crash

A simulated dataset of traffic collisions generated in CARLA using scenario_runner, where surrounding vehicles observe each incident from multiple vantage points. Every scenario records synchronized multi-camera and LiDAR sensor data from both the colliding and surrounding vehicles, providing multi-viewpoint sensor evidence suited to incident investigation as well as autonomous-driving research.

Data Format

Cameras

6× RGB cameras (front, front-left, front-right, back, back-left, back-right), 1600×900, record every 1 second

LiDAR

360-degree view top-mounted LiDAR, record every 1 second

Drag to orbit

Calibration

Per-frame calibration (.pkl): the vehicle's world pose, all 6 camera intrinsics, all 6 LiDAR-to-camera extrinsics, and the LiDAR-to-vehicle mount transform
