Overview

ScarCrash is a synthetic dataset of multi-vehicle traffic collisions generated in the CARLA simulator using scenario_runner. It provides three hand-built collision scenarios, each populated with several deliberately colliding vehicles and roughly 30 background traffic vehicles driving normally nearby.

For every recording, sensor data is captured from a single chosen vehicle at a time: six synchronized surround-view RGB cameras, one top-mounted LiDAR, and a combined per-frame calibration file, in a layout compatible with the DeepAccident dataset format. Because any vehicle in the scene -- the observing ego vehicle, either colliding vehicle, or any background vehicle -- can be selected as the recording target, the same collision can be captured from many different vantage points across separate runs.

Scenarios

Town03

5-Way Signalised Intersection Collision

Two vehicles collide at a five-way signalised intersection, surrounded by ~30 background vehicles driving normally nearby.

  • 2 deliberately colliding vehicles
  • ~30 background traffic vehicles
  • Ends automatically 2 seconds after the collision is detected
  • 6 witness vehicles + both colliding vehicles have full 6-camera + full 360-degree LiDAR recordings in the interactive viewer
Open Interactive Viewer
Town04

4-Way Unsignalised Intersection Collision

Three vehicles -- a red sedan, a green hatchback, and a truck -- converge and collide near a four-way unsignalised intersection, surrounded by ~30 background vehicles.

  • 3 deliberately colliding vehicles
  • ~30 background traffic vehicles
  • Ends automatically 2 seconds after the collision is detected
  • 6 witness vehicles + 2 of the 3 colliding vehicles have full 6-camera + full 360-degree LiDAR recordings in the interactive viewer
Open Interactive Viewer
Town04

Stationary Hazard Collision

A stationary vehicle sits as a hazard in the road; a firetruck drives into it, surrounded by ~28 background vehicles driving normally nearby.

  • 2 deliberately colliding vehicles (a stationary hazard + the firetruck)
  • ~28 background traffic vehicles
  • Ends automatically 0.5 seconds after the collision is detected
  • 6 witness vehicles + both colliding vehicles have full 6-camera + full 360-degree LiDAR recordings in the interactive viewer
Open Interactive Viewer

Every vehicle in the scene can be picked as the recording target at launch time, no code changes required -- see the Documentation for details. One more scenario is planned and will be added here later.

Data Format

Each recording produces, for the one chosen vehicle:

  • 6× RGB cameras (front, front-left, front-right, back, back-left, back-right), 1600×900, synchronized frame-by-frame
  • 1× top-mounted LiDAR, configurable point density and range
  • Combined per-frame calibration (.pkl): the vehicle's world pose, all 6 camera intrinsics, all 6 LiDAR-to-camera extrinsics, and the LiDAR-to-vehicle mount transform -- matching the DeepAccident calibration layout

Full field-by-field documentation, including the exact folder layout and array shapes, is in the repository README.
